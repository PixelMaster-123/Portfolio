Paper Publications & Research Studies

Peer-reviewed research and applied engineering studies covering enterprise data interoperability in Industry 4.0, natural fiber polymer composites for green automotive structures, and systems engineering for manufacturing.

Paper 01: Study on JSON, its Uses and Applications in Engineering Organizations
Authors Ishita Garg, Nagarjuna Reddy Wudaru, Pramod Ramakrishna
Discipline Industrial Digitalization, Cyber-Physical Production Systems (CPPS), Data Standardization
Research Scope Evaluation of lightweight data-interchange formats (JSON vs XML vs AutomationML) for agile data transfer across heterogeneous engineering hierarchies, from shopfloor CNC/PLC controllers up to enterprise PLM/ERP databases.
Key Findings Demonstrated superior parsing throughput, schema extensibility, and reduced communication payloads for JSON-based Asset Administration Shell (AAS) payloads in smart manufacturing environments.
Paper 02: Characterization of Novel Careya arborea Roxb. Fibers for Sustainable Polymer Composites
Authors Materials & Sustainable Manufacturing Research Team
Discipline Materials Science, Green Automotive Composites, Bio-Fibers
Experimental Methodology Chemical surface treatments (Alkali / NaOH, Potassium Permanganate KMnO₄, and Stearic Acid) to remove hemicellulose and lignin, followed by tensile testing, SEM morphological analysis, and thermogravimetric analysis (TGA).
Key Findings Treated Careya arborea fibers exhibited substantial improvements in interfacial matrix bonding and thermal degradation resistance, validating their viability as eco-friendly reinforcements for automotive interior panels and lightweight structural components.
Paper 03: Integration of SAP and MES Data-Streams to Efficiently Detect Scrap Costs
Author Pramod Ramakrishna
Institutions Robert Bosch GmbH & Otto-von-Guericke-Universität Magdeburg
Focus Area Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE), Digital Plant Topology, Manufacturing Accounting
Contribution Constructed a complete semantic bridge between shopfloor sensor events and financial cost-center allocations in SAP ERP, dramatically shortening root-cause diagnosis timelines for defective workpiece batches.
